# Track 3 — SPEC89 scavenging and speed bridge

SPEC89 evidence is recovered from collectors, publications, Usenet and preserved
archives. [Curated snapshot](../spec89-curated-snapshot.md),
[Helman supplement](../spec89-helman-worksheet.md),
[hardware dates](../spec89-hardware-years.md), and
[early archive leads](../spec-early-archives.md) own those distinct topics.

The current [SPEC89 bridge report](../spec89-bridge.md) owns this route:

`SPECint89 → SPECint92 = speed-VAXen`.

The edge uses archived paired integer scores. VAX-11/780 is the SPEC92
reference machine, so SPECint92 is speed-VAXen without conversion. Since
2026-10-03 historical routes are anchored to this single-job quantity; the DEC
same-column SPECint92/rate pairs are a diagnostic, not a second edge. There is
no supported base-variant bridge. SPECmark89 and SPECfp89 do not substitute for
SPECint89. The old [89/92 scatter report](../spec89-cpu92-scatter.md) remains a
source-evidence review even though its plotting command was retired.

The resulting old-hardware estimate table preserves source scores and labels
inferred SPECint92 explicitly. Confidence bounds on the multiplier are not
individual prediction intervals. These estimates also supply the joint classical
model; that reuse is an explicit dependency, not additional independent evidence.
