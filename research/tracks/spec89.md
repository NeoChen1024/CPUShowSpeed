# Track 3 — SPEC89 scavenging and rate bridge

SPEC89 evidence is recovered from collectors, publications, Usenet and preserved
archives. [Curated snapshot](../spec89-curated-snapshot.md),
[Helman supplement](../spec89-helman-worksheet.md),
[hardware dates](../spec89-hardware-years.md), and
[early archive leads](../spec-early-archives.md) own those distinct topics.

The current [rate bridge report](../spec89-rate-bridge.md) owns this route:

`SPECint89 → SPECint92 → SPEC92 integer-rate VAXen`.

The first edge uses archived paired integer scores. The second uses distinct
speed/rate rows in DEC machine/OS columns. Their product estimates peak
rate-VAXen. There are no current direct 89/rate hardware pairs, and no supported
same-column base-rate bridge. SPECmark89 and SPECfp89 do not substitute for
SPECint89. The old [89/92 scatter report](../spec89-cpu92-scatter.md) remains a
source-evidence review even though its plotting command was retired.

The resulting old-hardware estimate table preserves source scores and labels
inferred rate explicitly. Confidence bounds on the multiplier are not individual
prediction intervals. These estimates also supply the joint classical model;
that reuse is an explicit dependency, not additional independent rate evidence.
