SPICE2G6, DUDOC, FPPPP, and SPECfp89 all show reasonably strong
correlations with one another. The correlations start to go to pot
with NASA7, then TOMCATV, and finally MATRIX300 which shows the
poorest correlation to the rest of the programs. These last three
programs appear to be the most sensitive to optimization. SPICE2G6
and DUDOC appear to be the most 'stable' programs (so far anyway).

Starting with system 061 the Arithmetic Mean starts to deviate
widely from the Geometric Mean and the Standard Deviation grows 
from +/-11 for system 061 up to +/-240 for system 075. It is with
these systems that the fluctuation in performance becomes most
erratic and hence the ability to predict performance differences a 
bit more fuzzy.
