The following numbers were taken from the HP 9000 Series 700 Performance
Report that was released in January of 1992. AIM results have been requested
here several times recently, so here are a few that were available about 3
months ago.  The new machines from Silicon Graphics and IBM were not 
available at that time.
***************************************************************************

-------------------------------------	but is usually lower than these #s)

IBM550		34.5
HP710C		29.6
