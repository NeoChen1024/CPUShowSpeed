1) Geometric Mean is the Nth root of the product of N terms.  E.G.
	GM(3,4,5,6) =  4.355877174693  = 4th root of 360

2) It is always best to publish elapsed times.  The rules for computing 
	a SPECmark require that you carry at least 4 decimal places in the
	SPECratio (even though only 1 decimal place is published), in order to
	guarantee accurate results.

3) To publish accurate SPECmarks, you need to compute the SPECratios
	against the VAX reference times.  Once again, the VAX times are carried
	to 1 decimal place in the spreadsheets which publish the data, even
	though no decimal places are shown in the SPEC newsletter publication.
	I will post a copy of the VAX reference times the next time that I am
	at my desk.

4) Due to the way in which some of the data in the prior posting was
	developed (e.g. derived from published SPECratios), be sure to treat
	them only as approximate.  The actual numbers will vary by +/- 0.2.
	The ultimate authority is always the SPEC newsletter.
